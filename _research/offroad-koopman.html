Terramechanics · Koopman MPC

Learning vehicle dynamics on deformable terrain

Terrain-dependent prediction and constrained control for off-road vehicles.

Control-oriented vehicle modeling.

The challenge

Deformable terrain changes the forces acting on a vehicle. A control model needs to capture these effects without repeatedly solving the full terramechanics model online.

Learning a control-oriented model

I learn terrain-dependent Koopman predictors from high-fidelity simulations and use them within constrained model predictive control. The study examines prediction accuracy, model complexity, and behavior across different soils.

Control and scope

The controller accounts for steering and torque limits, with optimization times of approximately 85 ms in the simulation study. The published study is simulation-based; a 1/10-scale vehicle is a separate extension toward physical testing.
