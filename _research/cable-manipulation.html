The task

Deformable cables and precision connector insertion require perception and manipulation to work together.

My work with Team ASIMOV

I worked on perception and manipulation workflows in the Intrinsic × Open Robotics AI for Industry Challenge. Our solution used Intrinsic Flowstate, vision-language-action models, reinforcement learning, and OpenCV-based perception.

Competition outcome

The team ranked 21st globally and advanced to the top 30 of more than 160 teams. This is a competition project, separate from the research publications.
