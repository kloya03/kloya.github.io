Roll stability of a hydrodynamic sleigh

The question

How does periodic propulsion affect the roll stability of a system moving through a fluid? A hydrodynamic Chaplygin sleigh offers a reduced model for investigating this interaction.

Analyzing the dynamics

I study parametric roll oscillations and the role of added mass. Floquet analysis is used to examine stability under periodic dynamics.

Connection to robotics

The work develops a dynamical perspective on the interaction between propulsion and stability in fish-like systems. It corresponds to the 2023 Nonlinear Dynamics paper.
