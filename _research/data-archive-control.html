The challenge

Data-driven predictive control depends on the observations available to describe system behavior. An archive provides a way to organize observations for modeling and control.

Connecting data and prediction

This work studies Koopman operator based predictive control with a data archive of observables. It connects the representation of dynamical systems with the data used to construct a useful predictor.

Place in my research

The project is part of my work on learning control-oriented dynamics and precedes the later investigation of online model updates from streaming data. The associated paper appeared in ASME Letters in Dynamic Systems and Control in 2023.
