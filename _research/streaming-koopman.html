The challenge

A model learned from one collection of trajectories may become less useful when the operating regime changes. Streaming measurements provide an opportunity to update the model.

Updating a dynamical representation

I study Koopman representations with recursive subspace identification and selective incorporation of informative data. The aim is to adapt predictive models efficiently as new observations arrive.

Research focus

The project examines learning from data across dynamical regimes. It is associated with the 2024 IFAC paper on online learning of the Koopman operator.
