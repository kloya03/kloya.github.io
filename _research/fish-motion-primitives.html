Agile swimming and motion primitives

Investigating three-dimensional maneuvers in a fish-like robotic platform.

Related fish-platform assembly footage; not a demonstration of the 3-D maneuvers.

Beyond planar swimming

Three-dimensional motion expands the range of maneuvers available to a fish-like robot. This collaborative project investigates agile motion primitives on the robotic platform.

The physical platform

Internal actuation, body geometry, and interaction with water shape the resulting motion. The related assembly video introduces the mechanical platform used in this broader research program.

Related research

This project corresponds to our paper, Agile 3-D Motion Primitives in a Fish-Like Robot, in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters. Dedicated maneuver footage can be added here when available.
