The challenge

An inverted pendulum is unstable near its upright configuration. Mounting it on a nonholonomic platform couples the stabilization problem to the platform’s constrained motion.

Modeling and control

This work considers stabilization using an internal reaction wheel and Koopman-based model predictive control. The focus is on controlling the coupled dynamical system.

Related research

The project connects nonholonomic mechanics and data-driven feedback control and is associated with the 2022 IFAC paper on stabilization of an inverted pendulum.
