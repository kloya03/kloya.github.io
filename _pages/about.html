About me

My name is Kartik Loya. My research at Clemson University focuses on robotics, nonlinear dynamics, and learning-based control. I work on methods that combine physics-based models with data to understand and control robotic systems.

My doctoral research with Prof. Phanindra Tallapragada in the Applied Dynamical Systems Lab spans two main applications: internally actuated fish-like robots and off-road vehicles on deformable terrain. I develop predictive models using Koopman learning and system identification, and use these models for state estimation and real-time model predictive control.

I enjoy building and testing robots as much as developing the algorithms behind them. My work includes mechanical design, embedded systems, sensor integration, simulation, and experiments in pool and lake environments. I am also interested in reinforcement learning, Physical AI, and transferring learned models and policies from simulation to hardware.

You can explore my publications or view my CV.
